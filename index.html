Paste a List of Students

One student a line. Add 1.5x or 2x after a name for extra time, "all in Testing Center" if they take all of it there, and "make-up" for a make-up (e.g. Caspian C., 1.5x, all in Testing Center). Students you've entered before keep their grade and extra time.

A line can name its own period instead (e.g. Ari B., 2x, D period).

Fill the Testing Center's Forms

The Testing Center's form takes one sitting at a time, so each sitting gets its own tab of the real form with its answers filled in. In each tab: check it, attach the test on the last page and press Submit. Don't reload a tab before you submit it (Google can put another tab's answers back in); if one shows the wrong student, click Clear form at its foot and open it again from here.

    Chrome opens one tab a click. Open next always works; Open all at once needs pop-ups allowed for this page (once).
